Fill repetitive Google Forms in one click

College placement drives ask the same questions over and over — name, mobile number, email, roll number, CGPA, skills, links. EzApply stores that once, locally, and fills any Google Form from the toolbar. It gets smarter the more you use it, and it never submits anything on your behalf.

How it works

flowchart LR A[Open Google Form] --> B[Click EzApply Icon] B --> C[Review Auto-Filled Details] C --> D[Submit Form]

One-click fill

Open any Google Form and click the EzApply toolbar icon. Your saved details are matched to the form's questions and filled in automatically.

It learns as you go

Correct an answer and EzApply remembers. Any question it doesn't recognise can become a permanent field of your own with two clicks.

You stay in control

Every fill is reviewed before you submit. EzApply never submits a form for you — you always send the final click.

Add your own fields

College forms invent questions the built-in fields can't cover — a LeetCode link, a hostel name, a blood group, a company-specific question. EzApply lets you add these in two ways:

flowchart LR A[Unrecognized Question] --> B[Skipped in Form] B --> C[Click '+ Add this field'] C --> D[Saved for Future Forms]

From the form

Find the question in the panel's Skipped list and click + Add this field. The name and type are pulled from the question itself — type your answer once and it fills that question, and every similar one, from then on.

Ahead of time

Go to the options page → My Fields → + Add field, and set it up before you even open a form.

Learns from corrections

Fix an answer by hand and EzApply reacts: it links the question to an existing field, offers to update that field, or offers to save a brand-new one — whichever fits. Everything learned is listed under What EzApply has learned, with a Forget button per row.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work on any Google Form?

Yes. EzApply reads a form's questions by their accessibility roles rather than Google's changing class names, so it keeps working even when Google updates the page's internal styling.

Where is my data stored?

Only in chrome.storage.local on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded, and the codebase makes no network requests at all.

Can I move my saved details to a new computer?

Yes — use Backup → Export JSON on the options page before reinstalling Chrome or switching machines, then import it on the new setup.

Install

  1. Download the extension using the button above, then unzip the folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in your browser.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right of the page).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  5. The options page opens automatically — fill in your details.
  6. Open any Google Form and click the EzApply toolbar icon.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your device. EzApply stores your details in chrome.storage.local, in your browser profile on your computer only. There is no server, no account, and no network request of any kind in the codebase. Back up your details any time from the options page under Backup → Export JSON.
